What I work on

01 / Research
01

Video-to-Audio Generation

Generating soundtracks that are semantically and temporally aligned to video — the right event, at the right frame, with the right texture.

02

Reasoning for Audio

Bringing chain-of-thought into multimodal LLMs so a model can decompose a scene before it generates or edits sound, instead of guessing in one shot.

03

Spatial & Immersive Audio

First-order ambisonics from 360° video, plus representation learning and structured regularization for audio autoencoders.
